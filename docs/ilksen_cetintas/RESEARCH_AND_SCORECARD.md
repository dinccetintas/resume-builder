## 3. Executive-CV best practice (evidence base)

- **Retained-search view:** first screen = titles, company names, dates, education; then a one-line
  company description, headcount/budget per role, quantified results, every role listed, gaps
  explained, CV consistent with LinkedIn ([Odgers Berndtson senior guide](https://www.odgers.com/media/10952/cv-in-the-digital-age_senior-guide.pdf));
  accomplishments over responsibilities, context for lesser-known employers, current role
  ≈75% of the space ([Korn Ferry](https://www.kornferry.com/insights/this-week-in-leadership/three-best-resumes)).
- **Scan time:** ~6–7.4 s initial screen; 80% of that on name, current title/company/dates,
  previous title/company/dates, education. Professional restructuring raised usability
  6.2/7 vs 3.9/7 ([Ladders 2012](https://www.bu.edu/com/files/2018/10/TheLadders-EyeTracking-StudyC2.pdf),
  [Ladders 2018 via HR Dive](https://www.hrdive.com/news/eye-tracking-study-shows-recruiters-look-at-resumes-for-7-seconds/541582/)).
- **What boards want from a CHRO:** succession pipeline, executive pay through the remuneration
  committee, culture as a risk issue, AI governance in HR
  ([Harvard Law School Forum / Conference Board](https://corpgov.law.harvard.edu/2025/05/08/the-evolving-role-of-the-chro-in-the-boardroom/));
  2026 CHRO priorities: AI-driven HR transformation, workforce redesign, leadership readiness,
  performance culture ([Gartner](https://www.gartner.com/en/newsroom/press-releases/2025-10-02-gartner-says-chros-top-priorities-for-2026-center-around-realizing-ai-value-and-driving-performance-amid-uncertainty)).
- **Length:** 3–5 pages common for senior executives (Odgers; [WittKieffer](https://api.wittkieffer.com/wp-content/uploads/2022/09/write-and-maintain-a-great-executive-resume-2.pdf)) → 3 pages, page 1 self-sufficient.
- **ATS:** 97%+ of the Fortune 500 run an ATS ([Jobscan](https://www.jobscan.co/blog/fortune-500-use-applicant-tracking-systems/));
  single column, no tables/text boxes, contacts in the body, standard headings, one date format;
  DOCX for portals and search firms, text-based PDF for people.
- **Age bias:** general advice says detail 10–15 years ([AARP](https://www.aarp.org/work/job-search/age-proof-resume-tips/));
  executive search says state every role (Odgers). Compromise used: full detail for all roles
  after 1996, TAI condensed under "Earlier Career", no "38 years" figure.
- **Photos:** expected by many employers in Türkiye ([Kariyer.net](https://www.kariyer.net/kariyer-rehberi/cvnde-asla-olmamasi-gereken-6-sey)) and the Gulf;
  optional in DACH; avoid in UK/US. → photo and no-photo PDFs.
- **Design:** single column, one accent colour, no skill bars/charts/icons/two-column templates
  ([Hiration](https://www.hiration.com/blog/skill-bars-resume/), Ladders 2018).
